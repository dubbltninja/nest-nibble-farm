Transparent timing

Same-Day Results

Fast answers without the long wait.

We offer fast, same-day DNA testing 7 days a week, unless otherwise communicated.

Samples received by 12:00 PM can generally be processed the same day, subject to our daily testing capacity of 16 samples.

Samples received after the cutoff, or after that day's testing capacity has been reached, will be processed on the next available testing day.

If same-day testing is not possible for any reason, we will communicate this with you so you know when to expect your results.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does testing cost?

DNA poultry testing is currently $5 per sample as part of our limited-time introductory pricing.

What birds can you test?

Male/female DNA testing is available for chickens, ducks, geese, peafowl, turkeys, guinea fowl, and some parrots.

What samples can I send?

A small blood spot on a coffee filter, a plucked feather, or membrane from the shell the bird hatched from.

Can I mail my samples?

Yes. Samples may be mailed or brought directly to the farm.

How do I get started?

Message Lindsey directly through Facebook to discuss your testing needs and receive instructions for submitting your samples.

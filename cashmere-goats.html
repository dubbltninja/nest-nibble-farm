Why Cashmere

A heritage fiber animal with a steady presence.

Cashmere is the soft undercoat these goats grow for warmth as the weather cools. The fiber can be collected, dehaired, processed, and spun into yarn for knitting, weaving, and other handmade projects. Cashmere goats grow that coat through the fall and winter, then naturally shed it in the spring, when it can be brushed out by hand instead of clipped with shears.

About the farm

Nest & Nibble Farm is a small business based in Henderson, Maryland, raising chickens, ducks, geese, and cashmere goats with practical care and respect for the animals.

Our approach

Built around ethical animal raising.

Lindsey and Tim have a love for animals and believe in raising them responsibly. We are a small farm, not a large hatchery, so availability may be seasonal and interest based. If a breed catches your eye, send an interest request or message Lindsey directly on Facebook.

Hands-on experience

Lindsey spent 9 years raising chickens, pigs, goats, steer, and market lambs through her local 4-H club.

Selective hatching

We may hatch birds when timing, availability, and customer interest make sense.

Health-minded standards

NPIP tested. Certification pending.
